# Kickskraze Return & Deadstock Policy

## 14-Day Return Window
Kickskraze offers a 14-day return window from the date of confirmed delivery for all eligible footwear and apparel items.

## Deadstock Condition Requirements
To qualify for a full refund or exchange:
- The sneakers must remain in 100% brand new, unworn, and undamaged condition (Deadstock).
- Original factory packaging, including shoebox, shoe trees, extra laces, and accessories, must be intact.
- The Kickskraze Tamper-Proof Authentication Tag must remain securely attached. If the tag is clipped, removed, or tampered with, the return is immediately void.

## Return Shipping & Processing
- Domestic return shipping labels can be generated via the Customer Order Portal.
- Once inspected and verified by our warehouse authentication team, refunds are issued to the original payment method within 3 to 5 business days.
