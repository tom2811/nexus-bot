# Kickskraze Authenticity Guarantee

## 100% Verified Authentic
Every item sold on Kickskraze is guaranteed 100% authentic. We do not tolerate replicas, unauthorized authentic pairs, or counterfeit goods.

## Multi-Point Physical Authentication Check
Prior to customer dispatch, every sneaker undergoes physical evaluation by trained sneaker authenticators:
1. **Material & Stitching:** Evaluation of leather grain, suede nap, panel stitching consistency, and factory scent.
2. **Typography & Labels:** Verification of inner size tags, box labels, font spacing, and barcode/QR verification.
3. **Ultraviolet (UV) Scan:** UV blacklight inspection for factory glue stamps, stitching guides, and hidden counterfeit indicators.
4. **RFID & NFC Verification:** Scanning embedded microchips on select Nike, Jordan, and Yeezy models against manufacturer serial numbers.

## Tamper-Proof Tagging
Once authenticated, a serial-numbered Kickskraze tag is sealed on the sneaker.
